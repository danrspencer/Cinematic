Hi! Cinematic already fades Bartender4 bars and brings them back on mouseover. Just point at where a bar sits and it fades in (the other bars in its group come with it).

If it doesn't happen:
- Open /cine, go to Visibility > Hover Groups and make sure "Enable Page" is ticked.
- If a bar stays faded even outside CineMode, that's Bartender's own "Fade Out" option in its bar settings. Turn it off and let Cinematic do the fading.

Your question also helped me find a bug: Bartender bars ignored their own Hover Groups rows, so a custom group (say, just one side bar) didn't work for them. That's fixed in the next update, so each bar follows its own row.

If it still won't come back, tell me which bar it is and I'll take a look.
