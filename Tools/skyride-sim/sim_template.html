  • Built from the real SkyrideCam.lua and CameraScripts.lua, on a stand-in game camera that does only what the commands sent tell it to. Speed settings are the game's defaults: 180° a second to turn, 90° to tilt, 20 yards to zoom.
  • Not modelled: steering with the mouse (it stops our turns and tilts), fights, instances, and whether the game takes turns and tilts in the air at all. Run 4's probes answer that.
  • A positive tilt lowers the camera, so it looks up at you, as the quest cam's Lower the camera does. The stand-in camera starts 15° above you, and on the ground it stops at ground level.
