Good idea, and it's in for the next update. On the Nameplates page's Names tab there's a new Service NPCs row, under Friendly NPCs. Tick where you want it (Open world, Cities...), then set Show to "Titles only" under the grid: NPC names stay hidden, and their titles like <General Goods> show where the names would be. Kinds lets you pick which titles count: vendors, repair, trainers, flight masters, innkeepers and so on.

One catch: further away than nameplates reach, the game draws the name and title as one piece and has no setting for the title alone. So far off, you'd see both or neither. Same in dungeons and raids.
