Not a noob question at all, and it gave me a good idea! The next update adds exactly that: vendors, trainers and other service NPCs keep their names in CineMode while guards and townsfolk stay hidden.

To set it up once it's out:

1. Open Cinematic's settings (/cine), go to Interface, then Nameplates (under Overhead).
2. Pick the Names tab. Under Friendly NPCs there's a new Service NPCs row: tick its box to keep it on everywhere, or use its dropdowns to pick where (open world, cities, in fights...).
3. Under the grid, Service NPCs has two more settings:
   - Show: "Names and titles" (Thomas, with <Repair Vendor> under it) or "Titles only" (just <Repair Vendor>, no name).
   - Kinds: which NPCs count. Vendors, repair, trainers, professions, flight masters, innkeepers, bankers and auctioneers, stable masters, and "Other titles" for everything else with a title (like <Captain of the Guard>). Untick that one to keep it to the useful ones.

Leave Friendly NPCs itself unticked, or every friendly NPC's name shows again.

One catch: beyond nameplate range, and in dungeons and raids, the game decides on its own: it shows the names of NPCs with something for you (vendors, quest givers...), with their titles.
