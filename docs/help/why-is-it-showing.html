Why is that on my screen?

One window lists every part of your interface, whether it's showing or hidden right now, why, and which settings say so. Or point at any frame and ask about just that one. No more guessing which tick or rule is keeping your minimap up.

Start here

The Why window

Type /cine why. A window opens that you can drag aside and keep up while you play. It lists every part of the interface Cinematic fades, and says in plain words what each is doing right now.

CineMode is on / off
The box at the top. If CineMode is off, Cinematic isn't hiding anything, and the box says what's keeping it off: a fight, a cast, an open window, a place it's turned off in, or the few seconds before it comes back. Click the box to open that setting.
Right now you are
What Cinematic sees you doing: in or out of combat, your target, where you are, your group, mounted. These are what the ticks and rules go by.
Each row
A part of the interface, as on the Game Frames page, with a colored edge: Showing (green), Hidden (red), Partly shown (gold) or Not on screen (grey), and the main reason under its name.
Not on screen
Cinematic isn't hiding it. The game just has nothing to draw there: no target, no group, or the frame is switched off in the game or by another addon.
Click a row
It opens to every reason (see what they mean). Click a reason to open its setting, Open settings for the row's page, or Show me where it is to flash a gold box on the frame, even one that's faded out.
Parts of a frame
A row that says parts opens to them too, each a row of its own under it with its own status and reasons: a unit frame's portrait, name, level, bars, icons and the rest (the Unit Frame Parts page's rows), the raid and party frames' names, role icons, buffs and highlights, and the minimap's header, clock and buttons. Click a part for why it's showing or hidden.
Search and filters
The search box above the list finds a frame or a part by name: type portrait or clock. All, Showing and Hidden filter the list, parts too, each with a count. Addons lists other addons' frames that Cinematic fades.
Rules
A row or part with a rule in place of its ticks shows the rule as its reason, word by word: green words are true now, grey ones aren't.
Pick a frame on screen
For anything you can't find in the list: starts Inspect, below. Stopping Inspect brings the window back.
Copy report
The whole list as text, to paste to us on Discord or in a bug report when you're stuck.

The list keeps itself up to date. Start a fight, target something or walk into a city with it open and watch the rows change.

Ask about one frame

Inspect

  1. Start it

    Click Pick a frame on screen in the Why window, or type /cine why pick. A bar at the top of the screen says Inspect is on.

  2. Point at a frame

    A gold box shows its name and whether it's showing or hidden. Faded-out frames count too: point where they'd be. While Inspect is on, pointing at a frame doesn't bring it up, so you see things as they really are.

  3. Click it

    A card opens beside it with every reason, and keeps itself up to date: start a fight or target something and watch it change. Click a line to open its setting. Click another frame to look at that one instead.

  4. Stop

    Right-click, press Escape, or close the card.

Reading the card

What the reasons mean

Shown because
What's bringing it up right now: a column ticked on its Game Frames row (Always, a place like Open world, In combat, the Calm Timer, a target column), a Keep open on the Minimap page, or a Hover group's event such as a minimap ping or quest progress.
Keeping it hidden
What's holding it back over everything else: the death camera, Chat Cam's Ultra immersive, your role, a fight's start, or the row it goes with being hidden.
Any of these would show it
Settings that are ticked but don't apply right now, like In combat when you're not fighting, or a place you're not in.
Rules
A row with a rule shows it word by word: green words are true now, grey ones aren't, so you can see which word is holding it back.
Not just the game's frames

Other addons, Cinematic's own, and tooltips

Another addon's frame
If Cinematic fades it, the card works as usual. If it doesn't, the box says Not faded and the card names the addon, with how to fade it too (/cine add, or the Addon Frames page). See Other addons.
Cinematic's own frames
Quest talk, off-screen bubbles, Chat Cam's subtitles, the letterbox, the minimap, Campfire and quest item buttons, Card Vision's cards and time of day say what they are and which page they come from.
Tooltips
A hidden tooltip can't be pointed at, so the Inspect bar has a Last tooltip button: it explains why the last tooltip showed or hid, by its kind (enemy, quest objective, gatherable...) and the Tooltips row's rule.
Everything at once

The Why Is It Showing? page

In the settings, under Community, the Why Is It Showing? page lists every row of frames, what it's doing right now and the main reason why. It updates while it's open.

  • Showing, Hidden and All filter the list, each with a count.
  • Sort puts it in order by name, or with the visible ones first.
  • Click a row for all its reasons and an Open settings button.
  • The Now line shows which situations are true right now (combat, world, city, group, mounted...): the same words rules use.
  • Pick a frame in game closes the settings and starts Inspect.
  • Open as a window closes the settings and opens the Why window.

The window, Inspect and the page cost nothing while they're closed: they only work while you're using them.
