Why is that on my screen?

One window lists everything Cinematic does: every part of your interface, the camera playing, the cards, the Visions, other addons. It says what each is doing right now, why, and which settings say so, and keeps a list of what just came and went. Or point at any frame and ask about just that one. No more guessing which tick, rule or trigger is behind it.

Start here

The Why window

Type /cine why. A window opens that you can drag aside and keep up while you play. Its tabs across the top cover everything Cinematic does (see the tabs), and each row says in plain words what it's doing right now. The first tab, Interface, lists every part of the interface Cinematic fades.

CineMode is on / off
The box at the top. If CineMode is off, Cinematic isn't hiding anything, and the box says what's keeping it off: a fight, a cast, an open window, a place it's turned off in, or the few seconds before it comes back. Click the box to open that setting.
Right now you are
What Cinematic sees you doing: in or out of combat, your target, where you are, your group, mounted. These are what the ticks and rules go by.
Each row
A part of the interface, as on the Game Frames page, with a colored edge: Showing (green), Hidden (red), Partly shown (gold) or Not on screen (grey), and the main reason under its name.
Not on screen
Cinematic isn't hiding it. The game just has nothing to draw there, and the row says what's missing where it can: No target, No focus, No pet out, You're not in a group. Otherwise the frame is switched off in the game or by another addon.
Click a row
It opens to every reason (see what they mean). Click a reason to open its setting, Open settings for the row's page, or Show me where it is to flash a gold box on the frame, even one that's faded out.
Parts of a frame
A row that says parts opens to them too, each a row of its own under it with its own status and reasons: a unit frame's portrait, name, level, bars, icons and the rest (the rows under each unit frame on the Game Frames page), the raid and party frames' names, role icons, buffs and highlights, and the minimap's header, clock and buttons. Click a part for why it's showing or hidden.
Search and filters
The search box under the tabs finds a row or a part by name on the tab that's open: type portrait or clock. The filters under it narrow the list, each with a count: on Interface, All, Showing, Hidden and Addons (other addons' frames that Cinematic fades).
Rules
A row or part with a rule in place of its ticks shows the rule as its reason, word by word: green words are true now, grey ones aren't.
Pick a frame on screen
For anything you can't find in the list: starts Inspect, below. Stopping Inspect brings the window back.
Copy report
Every tab as text, Recent too, to paste to us on Discord or in a bug report when you're stuck.
The line at the bottom
A short line on how to read the tab that's open. Point at it for more.

The list keeps itself up to date. Start a fight, target something or walk into a city with it open and watch the rows change.

Not just frames

The tabs

Rows on the other tabs say one of these, each with its color down the row's edge: On now (green), Ready for its moment (blue), Held back by another addon, a fight or CineMode (orange), Off (red), or nothing to show right now (grey). Open a row for every reason, each a link to its setting.

Interface
The frames Cinematic fades, as above. At its end: the game settings Cinematic changes, those it's holding now (what for, and to what) and the rest under Not in use now; then Quality of Life and the Wheels, on or off by their switches.
Cameras
Camera now: the camera playing and why, then every trigger that's on in its order, which one won, and why the others didn't (ranks lower, no camera set, held back by its own settings). Standing-still timer: how long you've stood still out of its wait, or what's pausing it (a flight, a window, a cast, a cutscene). Then each camera, and the Camera hub's pages (Scenes, Triggers, Skyriding Cam...).
Cards
Card now: the card on screen, those waiting their turn and those held through a fight. Then every card: ready if its moment came now, or why not (unticked, its Show in row, another addon's own), its In a fight setting and what it last did.
Visions
Every Vision and Atmosphere page, with its tabs under it: off and which switch did it, on now (low health, an emote card up, a quest giver talking, the letterbox up...), or ready and what it waits for.
Addons
Every loaded addon Cinematic knows: the frames of theirs it fades, the features you pick between on the Conflicts page (whose version is playing, and whether you picked it), and what it does for the rest. Then the addons it leaves alone, under Other addons loaded.
Recent
What came and went since you logged in or reloaded, newest first: cards shown, skipped, held, waiting or dropped, cameras starting and stopping, and your triggers held back or beaten, each with why. The answer to "why didn't my card show?". Its filters: Cards, Cameras, Skipped or held.
Ask about one frame

Inspect

  1. Start it

    Click Pick a frame on screen in the Why window, or type /cine why pick. A bar at the top of the screen says Inspect is on.

  2. Point at a frame

    A gold box shows its name and whether it's showing or hidden. Faded-out frames count too: point where they'd be. While Inspect is on, pointing at a frame doesn't bring it up, so you see things as they really are.

  3. Click it

    A card opens beside it with every reason, and keeps itself up to date: start a fight or target something and watch it change. Click a line to open its setting. Click another frame to look at that one instead.

  4. Stop

    Right-click, press Escape, or close the card.

/cine why pickStarts Inspect (type it again to stop)
Reading the card

What the reasons mean

Shown because
What's bringing it up right now: a column ticked on its Game Frames row (Always, a place like Open world, In combat, the Calm Timer, a target column), a Keep open on the Minimap page, or a Hover group's event such as a minimap ping or quest progress.
Keeping it hidden
What's holding it back over everything else: the death camera, Chat Cam's Ultra immersive, your role, a fight's start, or the row it goes with being hidden.
Any of these would show it
Settings that are ticked but don't apply right now, like In combat when you're not fighting, or a place you're not in.
Rules
A row with a rule shows it word by word: green words are true now, grey ones aren't, so you can see which word is holding it back.
Not just the game's frames

Other addons, Cinematic's own, and tooltips

Another addon's frame
If Cinematic fades it, the card works as usual. If it doesn't, the box says Not faded and the card names the addon, with how to fade it too (/cine add, or the Addon Frames page). See Other addons.
Cinematic's own frames
Quest talk, off-screen bubbles, Chat Cam's subtitles, the letterbox, the minimap, Campfire and quest item buttons, Card Vision's cards and time of day say what they are and which page they come from.
Tooltips
A hidden tooltip can't be pointed at, so the Inspect bar has a Last tooltip button: it explains why the last tooltip showed or hid, by its kind (enemy, quest objective, gatherable...) and the Tooltips row's rule.
Everything at once

The Why Is It Showing? page

In the settings, under Help, the Why Is It Showing? page is the same list as the Why window, as wide as the settings window. It updates while it's open.

  • The box at the top says whether CineMode is on, what's keeping it off if it isn't, and what you're doing right now, with a button that turns CineMode on or off.
  • The same tabs, each with its filters and a count on each, and the search box finds a row or a part by name.
  • Click a row for all its reasons, each a link to its setting, plus Open settings and Show me where it is. A frame with parts lists them under it.
  • Pick a frame on screen closes the settings and starts Inspect; Copy report gives every tab as text to send us.
  • Open as a window closes the settings and opens the Why window.
  • Its switch (on to start with) turns the page, the window and Pick a frame on screen off together, for anyone who doesn't want them.

The window, Inspect and the page cost nothing while they're closed: they only work while you're using them.
