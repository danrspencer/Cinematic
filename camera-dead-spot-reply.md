Thanks for the kind words, and for testing with and without the addon. You haven't missed an option, this is a bug in Cinematic.

Action bars 6, 7 and 8 sit in the middle of the screen, around your character. CineMode fades them out, but they still catch your clicks even though you can't see them, so the camera doesn't turn over that spot.

Until the fix is out, you can do either of these:

- Turn off Action Bars 6, 7 and 8 in the game's Options, under Action Bars, if you don't use them.
- Move them away from your character in Edit Mode.

Thanks for reporting it!
