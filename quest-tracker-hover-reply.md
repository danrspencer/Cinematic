Thanks! You can do both already:

- Quest tracker: on the Game Frames page, untick everything in the Quest tracker's row. It stays hidden in CineMode, in and out of fights, and hovering it brings it up, then it fades out after "Stays after mouseover" (Hover groups page).
- Bars 6, 7 and 8: on the Hover groups page, turn the "Action bars 6-8" group On (it's off to start with). Untick their rows on the Game Frames page too, and hovering any of the three brings them up.

For fights, keep "Stay in CineMode in combat" on (CineMode page, on to start with). Otherwise the whole UI comes back when a fight starts.
