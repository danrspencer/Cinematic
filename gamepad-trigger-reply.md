Thanks, glad you're enjoying it! This fell a bit short before. A button press brought your action bars up, but they faded after a set time even if you were still holding it, which doesn't help when you want to look over your bars. So we changed it in v2.0.31, and holding a gamepad button now keeps them up until you let go. To use it with just your trigger, open "Buttons that show them" on the Gamepad Frames page and leave only that trigger ticked.

Hope that helps, please let me know if it doesn't!
