Hi! All three:

Shortcut key: in the game's Key Bindings, under Cinematic, set a key for "Toggle CineMode". Press it to bring the whole UI back, press it again to fade it all out.

Hover: on by default. Hovering a faded frame brings it back, along with its group. To change the groups, type:

/cine find hover groups

To make hovering anything bring everything back, click the + there, make your own group and add all the frames to it.

Windows: in the next update, type:

/cine find opening windows

and tick "Opening windows" (bags, character, talents, professions, map and the rest) and "Talking to NPCs" (quest givers, vendors, bank, mail...). The whole UI comes back while one is open.
