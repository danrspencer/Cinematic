Thanks so much, glad you're enjoying it! And not your fault at all, that one's on Cinematic. What you're seeing is our "Custom dialogue frames" (the speech bubble over the NPC and the list of answers at the bottom of the screen). It's on in the Cinema profile. To make room for it, Cinematic hides the game's own gossip and quest windows off screen. Your d-pad still moves the selection in the game's hidden window, and our list doesn't show where it is. So you really are picking blind.

For now: open Cinematic's options, go to the Quest Vision page and untick "Custom dialogue frames" under "Talking to NPCs". The game's own windows come back with the controller highlight you're used to. If you only want the game's window now and then, there's also a "Show the game's window" link on our card that brings it back for that one conversation.

About Immersion: it hides the game's windows its own way, and Cinematic still thought one was open, so ours came up on top of Immersion's. The next update fixes that: with Immersion or DialogueUI on, only their window shows. Until then, unticking "Custom dialogue frames" gets you the same.

Both are a good fit for a controller: DialogueUI has its own gamepad mode (Xbox or PlayStation buttons in its settings), and Immersion works with a controller through ConsolePort. With DialogueUI, the next update also stops our quest camera from fighting DialogueUI's camera when a conversation starts. Until then, if the camera jumps around while talking to NPCs, turn off "Camera Movement" in DialogueUI's settings or the quest camera in Cinematic.
